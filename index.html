About Me

The Story
Behind the Lens

I'm a documentary and fine-art photographer based on the Pacific Coast. My work explores the intersection of light, landscape, and human presence — finding stillness in movement and stories in silence.

With over eight years shooting across 40+ countries, I've developed an eye for the extraordinary within the everyday. Whether it's dawn light over an alpine ridge or the quiet intensity of a market square, I seek images that resonate long after the shutter closes.

Available for editorial assignments, commercial projects, and fine-art print commissions.
